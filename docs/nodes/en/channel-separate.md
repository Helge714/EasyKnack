## Controls
| Parameter | Setting | Signal / condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stereo In | — | Audio; ±∞; Audio |

## Outputs
| Output | Signal |
| --- | --- |
| Left | Audio; ±∞; Mono |
| Right | Audio; ±∞; Mono |

## Behavior

[Guide](../../user/en/channels.md)
Left and Right expose the respective source side as mono, identical on both destination sides. A mono input appears at both outputs. Voice groups are preserved.
